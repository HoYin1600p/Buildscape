# BuildScape

BuildScape is a building and decoration mod made for Vault Hunters. It adds thousands of blocks, plus the tools and furnishings to use them: sleek tiles and rustic wood, glowing crystals, polished metals, living hedges, string lights, display pillars and much more. Everything is designed to sit comfortably next to vanilla Minecraft, so you can mix and match freely.

Whether you are raising a cozy village, a grand castle or a futuristic city, BuildScape gives you the materials to finish the job.

---

## Features

### Blocks for every style

- **Colored sand and sandstone** in eight colors, with smooth variants, slabs, stairs and walls.
- **Colored tiles and concrete** in all 16 dye colors, with slabs, stairs and walls.
- **Wood sets** such as Ashpen, bamboo and mangrove, with the full range of planks, fences, gates, signs and boats, plus wooden walls and display shelves.
- **Stone and natural variants** with slabs, stairs and walls for calcite, dripstone, tuff, deepslate, basalt, obsidian, dirt, moss, mud, snow, quartz and more.
- **Copper in every oxidation state**, including cut, chiseled, bulb, grate and slit variants. Copper oxidizes and can be waxed like vanilla.
- **Steel and cardboard families** with panels, mesh, grates, casings and many finishes.
- **Glazed and mosaic glass**, as blocks and panes, in all 16 colors.
- **Vertical slabs** for many of the block families.

### Nature and atmosphere

- **Hedges** for every tree type that connect to each other and can be walked through.
- **Layers** of wool, leaves and moss that stack like snow, and thin moss and snow overlays.
- **Rose vines**, **icicles** and **sulfur** formations for gardens, caves and winter builds.
- **Hollow logs and hollow steel pipes** that can be crawled through and climbed, and that can hold fluids.

### Decoration

- **Pillars** that join into columns and can display an item or a mob on top, with colorful particle effects.
- **String lights**, **big candles**, **decorated pots**, **jars**, **stars**, **ornaments** and **festive stockings** for every season.
- **Chains** in many materials, from copper to netherite.
- **Trophies** that celebrate your building milestones, and a dedicated **advancement tab** to guide you.

### Tools and helpers

- **Builder's Workbench and Builder's Pouch** for organizing and filtering your building materials.
- **Hammers and a wrench** for replacing and adjusting blocks.
- **Biome brushes** for working with biomes.
- **Stonecutter support** for converting between thousands of related blocks.
- An **in-game settings screen** for adjusting the mod to your taste.
- Works with **JEI** for browsing recipes.

## Installation

1. Install Minecraft 1.18.2 with Minecraft Forge for 1.18.2 (version 40 or newer).
2. Download the BuildScape mod file.
3. Place the file in your `mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and start building.

BuildScape is made to run alongside Vault Hunters, and it also works in a plain Forge 1.18.2 installation.
